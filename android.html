Ardaas for Android

Become a tester

Ardaas lets you write your own benti and read it within the Ardaas, in Gurmukhi, transliteration and English. Before Google will publish a new app on the Play Store, at least 12 people have to test it for 14 days. That's where you come in.

Before you start

Use the Google account that's signed in to the Play Store on your phone. Testing is linked to that account, so any other email address won't work.

Not sure which one it is? Open the Play Store app and tap your profile picture in the top-right corner. The email address shown there is the one to use.

You need an Android phone running Android 8.0 or newer. Have an iPhone? Ardaas is already on the App Store.

Three steps, about two minutes

1. Join the testers group

Open the Ardaas testers group, sign in with that same Google account and tap Join group. You won't get any emails from it, and members can't see each other.

2. Accept the test invitation

Open the test invitation and tap Become a tester. If it says the app isn't available, wait a few minutes after step 1 and try again.

3. Install Ardaas

From the invitation page, follow the link to Ardaas on Google Play and install it like any other app.

Then, please

  • Keep it installed for at least 14 days. Google counts testers day by day, so uninstalling early can restart the clock for everyone.
  • Open it a few times. Compose an Ardaas with your own benti, try Translate to Gurmukhi, and read it through.
  • Tell me what you think. Message me directly, or use Send private feedback on the app's Play Store page. Anything that's confusing, broken or wrong in the text is worth hearing about.

Privacy

Joining the group shares your email address with the developer only, and only so that Google can let you install the test version. It isn't used for anything else. The app itself has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and nothing you write leaves your phone. Full details are in the privacy policy. You can leave the group at any time once Ardaas is public on the Play Store.
